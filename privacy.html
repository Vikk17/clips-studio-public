ShadowVikk Clips Studio

Privacy Policy

Google and YouTube data

When the channel owner authorizes the connection, Clips Studio uses the YouTube Data API to identify the connected channel, upload approved clips, check their processing and visibility, and schedule and verify their release. It sends the approved video, title, description, audience setting and release time to YouTube.

Storage and sharing

Clips Studio stores authorization credentials in private server files and stores channel and video identifiers, release times and job status to manage the calendar and avoid duplicate uploads. It uses Google user data for these features. It does not sell that data or use it for advertising. Instagram and TikTok have separate publishing connections.

Revoke access or request deletion

The channel owner can revoke access in Google Account connections. To request deletion of stored connection data or ask about this policy, contact the operator through the ShadowVikk17 channel’s contact information. Videos already uploaded can be managed in YouTube Studio.
